MFC / Stages of chiropractic care

Chiropractic care plans & progress reviews

Your needs may change as symptoms improve. A useful care plan makes the next step clear and leaves room to reassess.

Call to schedule What to expect ↗

Address the current problem

Begin with an assessment and a discussion of the activities affected by pain. Agree on realistic goals, possible treatments, and when to review whether the plan is helping.

Build everyday function

As tolerated, work toward the movements that matter to you: walking, sitting at work, lifting, or returning to exercise. Home activity and exercise can be part of a broader plan, with guidance matched to your needs.

Plan for the longer term

Discuss what you can manage independently and when another visit would be useful. Ongoing appointments should have a clear purpose; a fixed lifelong schedule is not automatically necessary for everyone.

Make progress visible

Use the same everyday task at each review. For example, describe whether walking to the mailbox or completing a work shift has become easier. Improvement in one task does not guarantee a particular diagnosis or recovery timeline; it is a way to explain what matters to you.

  • Choose an everyday activity to track.
  • Share improvements and setbacks.
  • Review visit frequency and costs.
  • Reconsider the approach if progress stalls.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Will everyone follow the same stages?

No. These are general ways to discuss care, not a mandatory sequence. Your condition and preferences should guide the plan.
