A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

How do I make an appointment?

Call (804) 608-3045. The office team will help you find an appointment and answer questions about your first visit.

What should I do before my visit?

You can complete the fillable new patient paperwork ahead of time. Call the office if you have questions about coverage or your appointment.

Can I book a massage?

Massage and therapeutic stretching are available in 30- and 60-minute sessions for current chiropractic patients. Call the office for details and availability.
